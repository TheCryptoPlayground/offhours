When tokenized stocks trade

Average dollar volume in each half hour of the week, New York time. Darker means more trading.

    Daily volume by session

    Each bar is a US trading date. Overnight trading from 20:00 ET counts toward the next trading day; weekends and holidays appear under their own dates.

    Off-hours token volume as a share of US stock volume

    For each US trading date, divide off-hours token dollars by the recorded stock dollars for a fixed basket of securities observed in both feeds somewhere in the archive. Individual stocks can have missing observations on either side on a given date. Weekends and full market holidays are skipped.

    The five-day line divides the sum of token dollars by the sum of stock dollars over five consecutive US trading dates. Gaps on an open date indicate missing comparable volume. The stock feed misses overnight activity. This can raise the displayed ratio, especially with the off-hours denominator.

    When does token volume exceed stock volume?

    See coverage by source.

    Dollar volume measures the value traded. The period ratio divides total recorded token dollars by total recorded stock dollars. The date count measures how often token dollars were higher on dates with observations in both feeds. Missing stock observations do not count as zero-volume days. Coverage varies by stock, session and venue.

    Which market moves first?

    Each correlation compares one market’s half-hour price change with the other market’s change in the next half hour of the same session. Positive values indicate moves in the same direction. Values near zero indicate little linear association. Shared news can affect both markets.

    Do token moves line up with the next stock open?

    Explore the price chart and stock-by-stock results

    Each point is one stock over a closure of at least 20 hours. The token log return ends two hours before the overnight session resumes and weights each market by its recorded dollars. At least $10,000 in eligible token trades is required. The stock log return runs from its last eligible pre-closure bar close to the first regular-session bar open. Both use unadjusted prices, so corporate actions can affect the result.

    Coverage and downloads

    Coinbase-issued tokens on Base and Ondo-issued tokens on Ethereum are sampled for AAPL, AMZN, GOOGL, MSFT, NVDA, QQQ, SPY and TSLA where eligible pools exist. Weekly discovery selects up to two leading eligible pools per token by reported 24-hour volume. Each pool counts once. Issuer minting, redemptions and ordinary transfers are excluded.

    Data sources and APIs

    The dashboard publishes daily files that anyone can open without an account. The collector uses the following upstream APIs.

    • US stock volume. Alpaca supplies 30-minute price and share-volume bars from the consolidated US tape. We estimate dollars traded by multiplying shares by the bar’s volume-weighted average price, using its closing price when that average is unavailable. Alpaca historical stock bars.
    • Token trades on exchange order books. Historical candles come from the spot market APIs of Binance and Kraken. The status table below shows the latest collection result.
    • Token swaps on Base and Ethereum. GeckoTerminal supplies prices and dollar volume in 15-minute intervals for individual trading pools. CoinGecko supplies token lists and contract addresses used to identify those pools. GeckoTerminal API · CoinGecko API.

    Dataset scope

    • Spot trading of tokenized US stocks and ETFs on centralised exchanges with public market data (30-minute bars) and on DEX pools indexed by GeckoTerminal (15-minute bars). Quotes in dollar stablecoins count one for one as dollars.
    • DEX pools are grouped by what the token trades against. Scheduled discovery selects pools paired with dollar assets or major cryptoassets. The archive can also include stock-token pairs in headline totals. Other counterpart assets are counted separately.
    • The series excludes perpetual futures, issuer mints and redemptions, and trades outside the tracked order books and pools.
    • The stocks' own volume is the consolidated tape. Its overnight coverage is incomplete. Alpaca documents separate overnight feeds.
    • Exchange-reported spot volume is shown as published by each venue.
