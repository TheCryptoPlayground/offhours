    Dataset scope

    • Spot trading of tokenized US stocks and ETFs on centralised exchanges with public market data (30-minute bars) and on DEX pools indexed by GeckoTerminal (15-minute bars). Quotes in dollar stablecoins count one for one as dollars.
    • DEX pools are grouped by what the token trades against. Pools against stablecoins, majors (ETH, SOL, BNB, BTC) or other stock tokens make up the headline figures. Pools against anything else, mostly memecoins launched against stock tokens, are reported separately.
    • Not included: perpetual futures on stocks, which trade far more than spot tokens; trading inside apps that does not reach a public order book or chain; issuer mints and redemptions; venues without a free API.
    • The stocks' own volume is the consolidated tape. Before 6 December 2026 it does not comprehensively cover overnight ATS trading, although occasional eligible or late prints can appear after 20:00 ET.
    • Exchange-reported spot volume is shown as published by each venue.

    Download

    Data from the public market-data APIs of the exchanges listed above, GeckoTerminal and CoinGecko for on-chain pools and token lists, and Alpaca for the US consolidated tape.

    This dashboard is open source. See the GitHub repository for its code, data pipeline, tests and methodology.
